Good to know

The questions people actually ask at the counter.

Ground spices stay bright for 6 to 12 months; whole spices keep 2 to 3 years. Keep jars cool, dark and dry, and sniff before you cook — if the smell has faded, the flavor has too. Every batch is dated on the jar, and any jar that arrives stale gets replaced — just tell us.

Our own delivery covers Clarksville and the surrounding area. For anywhere else in the country, we ship by mail — spice jars travel well in our padded boxes, and shipping is free over $60 just like delivery.
